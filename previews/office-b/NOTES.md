**Preview B: 2D illustrated office.** This is a flat-vector office in a 3/4 top-down view, drawn with plain inline SVG and moved by plain JavaScript on `requestAnimationFrame`. It uses no libraries, no build step and no external requests (system fonts only). There are two files: `index.html` holds the HUD, CSS and layout (140 lines, 9 KB), and `office.js` holds the art, characters, engine and story (733 lines, 47 KB). Together they come to about 56 KB. People and glass fronts are sorted by depth every frame, so characters pass behind and in front of the glass correctly. Movement uses cubic ease-in-out along corridor waypoints, and limbs swing with the distance walked. Timers run on game time, so the story pauses with the tab. The story loops in about 90 s; this includes the 6 s auto-allow and two 4 s auto-merges, which also wait for the owner (the brief only asked for auto-allow). Pan and zoom work by drag, wheel and pinch on the SVG viewBox, with limits on the bounds; the zoom buttons include a fit button. On phones the right column becomes a bottom sheet with Activity and Details tabs that you can collapse. The view also starts zoomed in and slowly follows whoever is acting, until you drag or zoom yourself. **Not done:** the people do not find paths around each other (two people at the same spot can overlap for a moment).
